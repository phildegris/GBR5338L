The owner confirms **all original ANL and battery-post fuses were fitted** and a separate **70 A windlass breaker [CB-006](/about/configuration/equipment/cb-006/)** was retained. These are **protective components**, not separate remote switching contacts. They cannot be assigned to individual studs or protection positions from the 2025 photograph alone.
