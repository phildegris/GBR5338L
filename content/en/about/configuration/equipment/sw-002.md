## Switching and protection boundaries

**One physical switching component feeds two distinct functional circuits.** The outgoing House and Autopilot circuits remain separate in the Circuit Register and may have separate downstream protective devices, **but do not have independently controlled remote switches**. Their actual fused positions, protective ratings, cable routes and the switched-output branch point are unknown.

All five circuits originate functionally at [BB-003](/about/configuration/equipment/bb-003/), but the three bypass services must be wired upstream of the common remote contact. The exact busbar, fuseholder and switching contact interconnection has **not** been inferred from the historical photo.

The **BEP 701-MD** mentioned in 2026 planning remains a proposed alternative; do not confuse it with this owner-confirmed common-switch function.
