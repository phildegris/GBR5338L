## Manufacturer reference

**Identification confidence:** Candidate identification; the DC Logical Circuits source only says Starlink.

- Starlink Mini manufacturer sheet lists 12–48 V DC, 60 W input rating and typical average consumption of 25–40 W.
- Starlink’s optional USB-C PD power path calls for a 100 W, 20 V/5 A source (with the designated accessory).
- An independent 150 W PoE injector is listed in Ragtime’s source notes; this is **not** proof of compatibility or of the Mini’s actual onboard power architecture. Confirm hardware, connector, voltage and injector/splitter models.
