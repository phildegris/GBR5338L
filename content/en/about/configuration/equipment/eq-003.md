## Manufacturer reference

**Identification confidence:** Manufacturer and M510 model family confirmed by owner; exact variant and nameplate data pending.

- Manufacturer: Icom; fixed-mount Class D DSC marine VHF.
- Manufacturer specifications for IC-M510 list 13.8 V DC nominal (11.7–15.9 V), 25 W / 1 W RF transmit power and approximately 5.5 A current at 25 W transmission (variant dependent).
- Check the exact IC-M510 / IC-M510E / EVO version before using any fuse or power specifications for the installed device.
