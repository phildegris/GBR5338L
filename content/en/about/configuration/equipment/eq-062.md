## Manufacturer wiring reference

The [Lewmar HX1 manual, Issue 2](https://www.lewmar.com/mst_attachment/attachment/click/attachment_id/56/) shows a **manufacturer-supplied contactor, part 68000939**, with UP/DOWN switching and a **3 A control fuse** in the standard circuit. This is a useful **model to verify against Ragtime's installation**, not confirmation of the installed contactor part, fuse rating, foredeck control hardware or coil arrangement.

## Outstanding survey

Photograph contactor markings and terminals; identify battery/power contacts, motor contacts and control inputs; trace the two foredeck control wires, control protection, switch directions and any alternative control stations.
