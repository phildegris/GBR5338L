## Protection and connected equipment

The owner has previously confirmed that the [COM-frame four-way fuse board](/about/configuration/equipment/eq-057/) is fed from the original [DC panel](/about/configuration/equipment/eq-001/). The panel's `3,4` breaker is labelled **VHF AIS** and is rated **10 A** according to the owner.

**Working hypothesis — NOT yet wiring-confirmed:** `CB-003` may be the **upstream feeder breaker** to `EQ-057`. Until the conductor is traced, this relationship is **provisional**, not an established circuit-to-protection allocation.
