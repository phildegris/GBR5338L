### Visible layout

- **Lower left:** twelve circular control/breaker positions in a **three-column, four-row** grid, with adjacent red indicators and engraved function labels. Position numbers in older circuit notes are physical coordinates, not equipment IDs; actual ratings and mappings remain to be surveyed.
- **Upper left:** original-looking navigation-light mimic panel with yacht outline and four circular indicator/control positions; see [EQ-002](/about/configuration/equipment/eq-002/). Its electrical function is not verified.
- **Upper middle:** a rectangular meter or display and adjacent small control(s); exact functions unconfirmed.
- **Centre right:** a prominent MOODY-branded module containing a rotary selector. It resembles a multi-position battery selector, but the function and terminal arrangement must be confirmed.
- **Far right:** a round display/instrument and a white breaker or RCD-type device mounted on a black section. Exact electrical type, ratings and retrofit status unknown.

**Do not infer supply, switch contacts, polarity, protection ratings or cable paths from the faceplate photograph.**

## Manufacturer reference

The 1991 *Moody Yachts Owner's Manual* (Marine Projects (Plymouth) Ltd, Issue 3) covers the Moody 336, its 12 V battery system, a four-position battery isolation switch and an original-design **Moody 336 electrical installation diagram (printed page 5.5)**. The manual is a **historical design reference**, not confirmation of Ragtime's present wiring, breakers or modifications.

- [Moody 336 owner's manual — PDF](https://www.hisse-et-oh.com/store/medias/sailing/5dd/254/1c3/original/5dd2541c39819f3668e34fdf.pdf) — electrical system in Section 5; page 5.5 for the Moody 336 diagram.
- [Moody 336 owner's manual — HTML alternative](https://manualzz.com/doc/61289917/moody-336-owner-s-manual).

**Electrical system:** nominal 12 V DC according to the period owner manual; rated branch currents, trip characteristics, distribution architecture and actual battery-switch configuration remain unverified aboard Ragtime.
