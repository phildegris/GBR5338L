## Manufacturer reference

The 1991 *Moody Yachts Owner's Manual* (Marine Projects (Plymouth) Ltd, Issue 3) covers the Moody 336, its 12 V battery system, a four-position battery isolation switch and an original-design **Moody 336 electrical installation diagram (printed page 5.5)**. The manual is a **historical design reference**, not confirmation of Ragtime's present wiring, breakers or modifications.

- [Moody 336 owner's manual — PDF](https://www.hisse-et-oh.com/store/medias/sailing/5dd/254/1c3/original/5dd2541c39819f3668e34fdf.pdf) — electrical system in Section 5; page 5.5 for the Moody 336 diagram.
- [Moody 336 owner's manual — HTML alternative](https://manualzz.com/doc/61289917/moody-336-owner-s-manual).

**Electrical system:** nominal 12 V DC according to the period owner manual; rated branch currents, trip characteristics, distribution architecture and actual battery-switch configuration remain unverified aboard Ragtime.

## House-bank positive feeder (owner-confirmed architecture)

The owner identifies the main chart-table DC panel as the downstream endpoint of **House** service from the five-feed house-bank distribution [BB-003](/about/configuration/equipment/bb-003/). The House service is remotely switched through **House remote-switching function** (fitted hardware not identified) on [DC-HOU-01](/about/configuration/circuits/dc-hou-01/). The Autopilot has **a separate remote-switched distribution branch**, not an assumed subcircuit of House. The actual fitted remote switch model, feeder conductor size, protection and DC-panel input termination remain unverified. [Source topology](/about/configuration/house-bank-distribution/).
