**Corrected as-fitted connections (owner, 31 October 2026):** SW-001 switches **both** the **brown (`R3`) and white (`B4`) cores** of the 7 × 1.5 mm² Oceanflex trunk, which continue through the forecabin locker into a **separate Oceanflex 2 × 1.5 mm² mast cable** supplying NASA Combi EQ-021. **Neither switched leg is a permanently connected −12 V return**, including any black-insulated conductor in the two-core mast cable. The original handwritten `NC` marks for R3/B4 and old B2/Y3 DPDT assignments have been **superseded by the owner's clarification**; the source notes are preserved in the [connector schedule](/about/configuration/dc-terminal-schedule/).

The old drawing's switch pins 1–6 and mode truth table have **not** been continuity-tested; the physical mapping from individual mast-cable conductor colours to brown versus white is not recorded.
