## Manufacturer reference, not an onboard measurement

Lewmar's [HX1 installation, operation and servicing manual — document 65140050, Issue 2](https://www.lewmar.com/mst_attachment/attachment/click/attachment_id/56/) specifies for the **HX1 800**: nominal **800 W/12 V** model, **60 A current at working load**, a supplied **70 A breaker** (part 68000240), and **25 mm² cable guidance for a 20 m complete positive-and-negative electrical loop**. Its standard wiring diagram includes a **contactor** (part 68000939), **3 A control fuse** and **1.5 mm² control wiring**. These are **manufacturer specifications**; the owner's current confirmation is specifically the HX1 800 W, the fitted 70 A breaker and separate contactor with two foredeck switches. Do **not** infer that the installed contactor, fuse, switch parts or cable sections match the published standard drawing without tracing.

Lewmar advises a suitable independent means of securing the rode at anchor; the windlass should not serve as a permanent chain stopper. See the manufacturer's manual for operating and maintenance requirements.
