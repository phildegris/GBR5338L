Equipment records are listed in the [Equipment Register](/about/configuration/equipment-register/). **Status is generated directly from each equipment page**; the catalogue does not maintain a separate copy. `Mapped (U)` means owner-confirmed identity and connections are documented, **not** independently electrically surveyed. `U` = identified, `T` = tentative, `G` = grouped. Keep passwords and other credentials out of this public website.
