The owner confirms **one NASA Marine Combi LED** tricolour/anchor masthead fitting, selected by polarity through [SW-001](/about/configuration/equipment/sw-001/). In legacy notes, **B2 = blue-lever clamp in group 2** connected to the **blue-insulated** seven-core mast conductor, while **Y3 = yellow-lever clamp in group 3** connected to the **black-insulated** mast conductor. The lever colours are positional markers, not cable colour claims; the actual connections have not been continuity-tested. NASA Marine's *Supernova Combi* manufacturer instructions describe this polarity-switched configuration, but **the precise installed variant is not independently confirmed**. [NASA Marine fitting instructions PDF](https://www.nasamarine.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Super-Nova.pdf) (reference, not as-fitted proof).

## Manufacturer reference

**NASA Marine combined tricolour/anchor LED confirmed by owner**; the specific installed model variant is not yet established. The [NASA Supernova Combi product reference](https://www.nasamarine.com/product/supernova-combi-tri-and-anchor-l-e-d-mastlight/) and [fitting instructions (PDF)](https://www.nasamarine.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Super-Nova.pdf) document two-wire polarity-mode selection. The fitting instructions call for a 1 A fuse; Ragtime's reported 10 A panel breaker does **not** prove presence or absence of smaller downstream protection, or imply cable adequacy.
