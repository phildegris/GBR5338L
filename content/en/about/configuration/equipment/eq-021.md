The owner confirms **one NASA Marine Combi LED**, supplied by a **separate Oceanflex 2 × 1.5 mm² mast cable** with **two active polarity-reversible conductors**. The two mast cable cores join **R3/brown and B4/white** of the **Oceanflex 7 × 1.5 mm² DC-panel-to-forecabin trunk**, and both connect to [SW-001](/about/configuration/equipment/sw-001/) via DC-panel cage clamps. The exact connection of each *individual* mast cable core to brown versus white at the forecabin junction has not been recorded. The two NASA lamp wires, **including its black core**, must **not** be described as a permanent negative-busbar connection, because SW-001 reverses their polarity.

**Historical evidence, superseded:** The earlier DPDT sketch listed **B2/blue and Y3/black** as the masthead pair. The photographed handwriting originally marked **R3/brown and B4/white as NC** in the CB column. The owner has now explicitly corrected the as-fitted masthead pair to **R3/B4**, superseding both old interpretations without erasing the historical source. The 7-core **Y3/black** conductor's possible connection to a common negative busbar remains a **separate, unverified assumption**. [Connector schedule](/about/configuration/dc-terminal-schedule/). NASA Marine's *Supernova Combi* instructions describe this polarity-switched design; the exact installed model suffix remains unverified. [NASA fitting PDF](https://www.nasamarine.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Super-Nova.pdf).

## Manufacturer reference

**NASA Marine combined tricolour/anchor LED confirmed by owner**; the specific installed model variant is not yet established. The [NASA Supernova Combi product reference](https://www.nasamarine.com/product/supernova-combi-tri-and-anchor-l-e-d-mastlight/) and [fitting instructions (PDF)](https://www.nasamarine.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Super-Nova.pdf) document two-wire polarity-mode selection. The fitting instructions call for a 1 A fuse; Ragtime's reported 10 A panel breaker does **not** prove presence or absence of smaller downstream protection, or imply cable adequacy.
