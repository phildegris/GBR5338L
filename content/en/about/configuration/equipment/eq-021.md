## Known or reported relationships

- [`CB-001`](/about/configuration/equipment/cb-001/) — reported breaker 1,1
- [`SW-001`](/about/configuration/equipment/sw-001/) — reported polarity selector
- [`TB-001`](/about/configuration/equipment/tb-001/) — reported B2
- [`TB-002`](/about/configuration/equipment/tb-002/) — reported Y3

The owner confirms **one NASA Marine Combi LED** masthead fitting selected by polarity through [SW-001](/about/configuration/equipment/sw-001/). The handwritten [panel connector schedule](/about/configuration/dc-terminal-schedule/) identifies **B2 → Oceanflex blue** and **Y3 → Oceanflex black** cores of one **7 × 1.5 mm² cable running to the forecabin starboard locker**. A separate onward cable must carry power toward the mast via the swan neck. The older DPDT notes associate Y3 with one NASA lamp conductor, while the handwritten CB column marks Y3 as **NC**. These statements are retained without equating NC to a disconnected cable; the forecabin and DPDT junctions remain untraced. NASA Marine's *Supernova Combi* manufacturer instructions describe this polarity-switched configuration, but **the precise installed variant is not independently confirmed**. [NASA Marine fitting instructions PDF](https://www.nasamarine.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Super-Nova.pdf) (reference, not as-fitted proof).

## Manufacturer reference

**NASA Marine combined tricolour/anchor LED confirmed by owner**; the specific installed model variant is not yet established. The [NASA Supernova Combi product reference](https://www.nasamarine.com/product/supernova-combi-tri-and-anchor-l-e-d-mastlight/) and [fitting instructions (PDF)](https://www.nasamarine.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Super-Nova.pdf) document two-wire polarity-mode selection. The fitting instructions call for a 1 A fuse; Ragtime's reported 10 A panel breaker does **not** prove presence or absence of smaller downstream protection, or imply cable adequacy.
