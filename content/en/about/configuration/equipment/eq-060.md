## Engineering and evidence notes

The shunt measures **net battery current** only when all house-bank load and charging returns pass through the load/system side. Battery negative is the sole connection class allowed on its battery side; do not add a return bypass. The BMV-712 model is owner-confirmed; actual shunt rated current not read from fitted label.

**Keep distinct:** House-bank internal composition and planned LiFePO4 replacement are **outside this source-interface record**. No proposed product, fuse size, cable gauge, or switching capacity should be labelled installed without independent evidence.
