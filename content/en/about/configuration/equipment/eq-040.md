## Manufacturer reference

**Identification confidence:** Model explicitly recorded in the source page; installation not independently surveyed.

- Manufacturer input: 9–30 V DC via a 4-pin industrial connector.
- Manufacturer datasheet lists idle consumption under 3 W and maximum under 6 W.
- One gigabit Ethernet port; the source page refers to a separate 30 W PoE injector. **Do not assume that the TRB500 itself accepts IEEE PoE:** verify the exact injector, splitter and cable arrangement.
