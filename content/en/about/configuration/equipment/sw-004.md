> **Owner-confirmed physical control:** Ragtime has **two separate windlass switches mounted on the foredeck**. This is **switch B**, a stable audit identifier until the two physical positions can be distinguished. Both act on the **[separate contactor EQ-062](/about/configuration/equipment/eq-062/)**, which controls the [Lewmar HX1, EQ-061](/about/configuration/equipment/eq-061/).
