## Manufacturer reference

**Identification confidence:** ATB1 identity and COM-frame installation confirmed by owner; serial number and wiring pending.

- Manufacturer: Ocean Signal; Class B SOTDMA AIS transponder, with built-in GNSS.
- Vendor manual describes a 12/24 V DC installation with NMEA 2000, NMEA 0183, USB and Wi-Fi connectivity.
- The manufacturer’s installation illustration calls for a 3 A fast-acting fuse. **This is manufacturer guidance, not an assertion about the fuse installed on Ragtime.**
- An appropriate active AIS/VHF splitter is required for shared-antenna operation; owner confirms the fitted [Digital Yacht SPL2000](/about/configuration/equipment/eq-005/).
