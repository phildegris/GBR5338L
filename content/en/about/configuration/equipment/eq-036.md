## Manufacturer reference

**Identification confidence:** Model explicitly recorded on the source page; no serial or installed-wiring verification.

- Manufacturer input: 9–50 V DC through a 4-pin industrial power connector.
- Manufacturer lists idle consumption below 4 W and maximum consumption below 16.5 W; its measured power-consumption table varies with supply voltage and activity.
- Manufacturer supports **passive** power over LAN1 (9–50 V, Mode B), not standards-compliant IEEE 802.3af/at/bt PoE. Check what powers Ragtime’s unit.
- 5 gigabit Ethernet interfaces and dual SIM capability are model-level features, not proof that both SIMs are configured.
